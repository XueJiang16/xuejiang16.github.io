<div class="intro-block" markdown="1">

Hi, I am **Xue Jiang**, a joint Ph.D. student at the Trustworthy Machine Learning and Reasoning (TMLR) Group, Department of Computer Science, Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by [Prof. Bo Han](https://bhanml.github.io/), and Southern University of Science and Technology, advised by [Prof. Feng Zheng](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/?tagid=fengzheng&iscss=1&snapid=1&orderby=date&go=1&lang=en). In the TMLR Group, I am also co-supervised by [Prof. Feng Liu](https://fengliu90.github.io/index.html) and work closely with [Dr. Zhen Fang](https://fang-zhen.github.io/). From Feb. to Aug. 2025, I visited [RIKEN AIP](https://www.riken.jp/en/research/labs/aip/), working with [Prof. Masashi Sugiyama](https://www.ms.k.u-tokyo.ac.jp/sugi/) and [Dr. Gang Niu](https://niug1984.github.io/).

My research focuses on building **reliable and trustworthy machine learning systems**, with particular interests in **out-of-distribution detection**, as well as **hallucination mitigation** and **safety** for multimodal large language models.

Please feel free to [email me](mailto:csxjiang@comp.hkbu.edu.hk) for research, collaborations, or a casual chat.
